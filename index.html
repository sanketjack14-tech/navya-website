Product Capabilities

What NavyaINTEL Solves

Real screens from the Navya platform built for modern hiring teams.

Visual Hiring Pipeline

Track candidates across customized stages with full drag-and-drop clarity.

  • Kanban-style candidate movement: Drag candidates from Application to Screening, Domain Round, HR, and Hired.
  • Real-time stage tracking: Clear visibility on where every candidate stands in the funnel.
  • Status tagging: Filter by notice period, interview rounds, and priority candidates.

Hiring Decisions, Augmented by AI

Evaluate candidates accurately using 20+ match parameters without bias.

  • AI Match Score: Surfaces candidate-role relevance early to speed up shortlisting.
  • AI-Powered JD Creation: Creates role-aligned job descriptions with consistency across teams.
  • Intelligent CV Parsing: Structures unstructured CV data into usable hiring signals automatically.

Complete Candidate 360 View

All candidate information, resume preview, and interaction history in one clean tab.

  • Resume parsing & preview: View CV text and PDF side-by-side with structured experience.
  • Stage history & interview notes: Keep all panel notes and feedback standardized.
  • Google Calendar integration: Quick 1-click interview scheduling with hiring managers.

Smart Job Management

Define interview stages, assign panel members, and manage requirements easily.

  • Create & manage JDs: Compensation bands, working mode, and required qualifications.
  • Define interview rounds: Application → Screening → Technical → HR with custom feedback forms.
  • Assign hiring managers: Clear ownership and accountability for every role.

Actionable Hiring Analytics

Brings hiring data into one structured system to identify bottlenecks early.

  • Action Center: Flag interviews to schedule, pending feedback, and stuck candidates.
  • Candidate Funnel & Sourcing Breakdown: See volume across LinkedIn, Naukri, and Referrals.
  • Stage-wise aging analysis: Measure how long candidates spend in each stage to reduce delays.

Track Employee Referrals

Empower employees to submit candidates and view progress with full transparency.

  • Dedicated referral submission portal: Simple 1-click submission for staff.
  • Referral tracking dashboard: Real-time visibility into referee review stages.
  • Boost employee engagement: Eliminate inquiries about referral statuses.

Centralized Command Dashboard

Start each morning with clarity: Today's Hustle, upcoming interviews, and job approvals.

  • Centralize candidates: One system for all resumes and applicants.
  • Today's Hustle queue: Review newly assigned jobs and ready screenings.
  • Upcoming interview calendar: Never miss a candidate interview or hiring milestone.

About Us

Built by People Who’ve Lived Hiring Decisions
